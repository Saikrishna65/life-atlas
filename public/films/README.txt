Life Atlas illustrative motion studies

mountain-study.mp4 and coastal-study.mp4 are six-second silent animated
landscape studies, derived with FFmpeg from the existing mountains.webp and
ocean.webp assets. They are demonstrations, not recorded travel footage.
Source and license: src/data/demo/SOURCES.md in the repository and /images/world/LICENSES.txt.
No soundtrack. H.264, 960 x 600, 25 fps; playback is visitor-initiated.
Original published user media is never processed through this demonstration pipeline.
