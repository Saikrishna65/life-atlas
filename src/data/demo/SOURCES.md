# Public demonstration provenance

All journal entries, itineraries, costs, milestones, catalog artists, albums,
tracks, playlists, conversations, workflows, transactions, reminders and activity
are fictional authored demonstration data. No private application store is used.
Dates are fixed and ordering is deterministic. Budgets are examples, not quotes.
Travel information requires current local confirmation. Approximate geographic
centers are rounded to 0.1 degree and are not route or safety waypoints. Geographic
reference: https://www.geonames.org/ (CC BY 4.0); destination centers are broad
orientation aids. Their regional plausibility was cross-checked against the
GeoNames-derived gazetteer at https://github.com/lutangar/cities.json on
2026-11-12. `coordinate-references.json` records the nearest gazetteer settlement
and distance for each of the 36 centers. This check does not establish exact
landmark or trail locations; remote regions may have sparse settlement coverage.

Source repositories distribute assets under MIT; retained license notices are
in public/images/world/LICENSES.txt. These are illustrative landscape studies,
not photographs of the named destinations or real personal travel records.
The 180 gallery metadata entries describe a fictional collection backed by six
reusable image studies, not 180 distinct photographs. Album artwork is original
procedural CSS geometry. No audio recordings are distributed or played.

The social preview at public/og-image.jpg is a resized derivative of the existing
public/images/hero-backdrop.jpg. Its original file remains unchanged.
